# THE DRAPER — Advisor Character Specification

## Canonical Reference

`/public/brand/character/the-draper-advisor-master-reference-v1.jpg`

This is the canonical visual identity for the recurring THE DRAPER advisor.

When generating new imagery, use the master reference as the definitive identity reference. The character must remain recognisably the same person.

## Identity

Indian/South Asian male, early-to-mid 30s.

Appearance and personality:
- Handsome but understated
- Approachable
- Intelligent
- Calm
- Warm
- Trustworthy
- Tasteful
- Confident without being arrogant
- Professional without looking corporate
- Naturally friendly rather than sales-oriented

## Physical Consistency

Preserve the reference character's:
- facial identity
- facial proportions
- eyes
- nose
- jawline
- smile
- skin tone
- hairstyle
- stubble
- body proportions
- overall build

Do not beautify, age, masculinise, feminise or reinterpret the character.

## Signature Uniform

Polo:
Deep Olive, approximately #45483A. Premium matte cotton pique. Tailored but relaxed fit. Classic understated polo collar.

Trousers:
Light warm khaki / sand. Tailored straight or slim-straight fit. Avoid yellow/mustard tones.

Shoes:
Minimal clean white sneakers with no prominent visible branding.

Watch:
Cognac/warm tan leather strap, simple brushed stainless-steel case, clean ivory/light dial, approximately 38–40 mm, understated design.

Branding:
Official THE DRAPER D + flowing fabric monogram on the left chest, approximately 2–2.5 inches wide when visible. Use the supplied official logo artwork as the reference.

Back:
Clean for the hero/brand advisor. No large back print unless specifically requested for a field-staff uniform.

## Character Direction

The advisor should feel like a knowledgeable interiors professional whom a customer would comfortably invite into their home — not a conventional salesperson, corporate representative or fashion model.

## AI Generation Rule

Reference = WHO the advisor is.
Prompt = WHAT the advisor is doing.

Do not attempt to recreate the advisor from text alone when the master reference is available.

For every new AI image, attach the master reference whenever possible. Attach the official monogram separately whenever the monogram is visible.

Do not let the image model redraw, reinterpret, replace or invent a different version of the monogram.
