# THE DRAPER — Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name

THE DRAPER

## Primary Tagline

YOUR HOME. YOUR STYLE. OUR EXPERTISE.

## Service Proposition

THE SHOWROOM COMES HOME.

## Brand Idea

Expert home-furnishing advice, brought home.

## Positioning

THE DRAPER is a home-consultation brand. The showroom comes to the customer's home, where they can see fabrics, textures, colours and window-covering options in their own light and surroundings, receive expert advice, get measured and move through the process to professional installation.

The brand should feel personal, considered, calm and expert — never pushy or like a conventional showroom salesperson.

## Typography

Primary editorial serif: Cormorant Garamond
Supporting sans: Manrope

Use the serif for major editorial headings and the sans for navigation, body copy, buttons, labels and functional information.

Do not introduce additional primary typefaces without explicit approval.

## Logo System

Canonical master assets are stored in:

`/public/brand/logo/`

Master monogram:
`the-draper-monogram-master.png`

Master primary logo:
`the-draper-primary-logo-master.png`

The D + flowing fabric monogram is the signature brand mark. It must not be redrawn, distorted, simplified or replaced by AI-generated approximations.

If vector versions are created, they must faithfully reproduce the supplied master artwork and should be treated as derivatives of the master, not as a new design.

## Photography Direction

- Warm natural daylight
- Authentic contemporary Indian homes
- Editorial but lived-in
- Natural materials and real interiors
- Soft ivory, taupe, olive, wood and warm neutral palette
- Photorealistic
- Approachable rather than staged
- Premium but not flashy
- Avoid generic European/Dubai luxury imagery
- Avoid excessive HDR, plastic skin and fashion-editorial posing

## Brand Language

Preferred vocabulary:
considered, personal, expertise, at home, texture, light, craft, made-to-measure, thoughtful, beautifully finished, your space, your style.

Avoid overusing:
luxury, premium, best, No.1, affordable, exclusive, world-class.

## Website Principles

The website should feel like a premium interiors journal rather than a generic home-services website.

Use generous whitespace, warm ivory backgrounds, editorial photography, restrained typography, thin rules and simple navigation.

Do not introduce generic rounded-card/gradient UI patterns unless explicitly requested.
