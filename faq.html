FAQ · Mysore

Questions, answered.

The things people usually ask before booking a home visit with Drape at Home.

Do you charge for the home visit?

No. The home visit is completely free, comes to you, and carries no obligation to book anything afterwards.

How long does made-to-measure take, from home visit to installation?

Typically 2 to 4 weeks, depending on the fabric and the scope of the order.

What areas do you serve?

We serve homes across Mysore and Mysuru, Karnataka.

Will I see real fabric before I decide?

Yes. We bring a portable rack of real fabric samples to your home, so you're comparing actual curtains and blinds in your own light, not swatches or photos.

Do you handle both measuring and installation?

Yes. Advisors, tailors and installers work together as one team, from the first home visit through to final installation.

Can I get curtains, blinds and motorisation from one home visit?

Yes. One home consultation can cover curtains, blinds and motorisation together, room by room — see our combined curtains & blinds service for Mysuru.

Still have a question?

Ask us directly, or book a free home visit to see everything in person.
